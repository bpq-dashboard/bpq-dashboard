Looking for the modems…

Each modem's own display, without opening the station's desktop. VARA HF, flrig and the other programs with a window are shown as a picture of that window, taken on the station once a second or two; you can look but not click. Sound modems that run without a window (QtSoundModem with nogui, Dire Wolf) get a status panel: the program, whether LinBPQ is connected, and the stations heard today. Mercury's and Data2G's monitors are redrawn here from what the modem sends, through a watch-only relay that passes no command back. A panel only updates while it is on screen.
